# Brand brief — DEMO

Fallback material so you can run a workflow end to end before your own brand
brief is ready. Replace it with the real thing as soon as you can — the output
is only as specific as this file.

## Audience type
B2B / sales-led — we want replies and conversations.

## Who, specifically
Solo founders and two-person teams who have shipped a product in the last six
months and have fewer than 100 users. Mostly technical, mostly in Europe.

## What I sell
A £19/month tool that turns a founder's changelog into social posts.

## The promise
You stop disappearing for three weeks between launches.

## Proof
340 users. Average customer posts 4x more after week two.

## Voice
**Three words I am:** direct, specific, unbothered
**Three words I'm not:** corporate, breathless, salesy

## Never say
"Revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x", "in today's fast-paced world",
"AI-powered" as a selling point, any growth claim without a number behind it.

## Formatting rules
Sentence case headings. No emoji. First person singular. Em dashes are fine.

## What I've learned
- Posts that name a real number get 3x the replies of posts that don't
